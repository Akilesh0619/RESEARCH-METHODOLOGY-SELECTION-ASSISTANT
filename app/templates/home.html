How MethodAssist Works

A three-stage transparent pipeline designed for academic integrity

1. Study Questionnaire

Answer an 11-dimension survey about your study's objectives, data type, participant access, sample size, time, and budget.

2. Rule Engine Scoring

An external declarative ruleset computes weighted boosts, penalties, and hard constraints to calculate normalized match percentages with detailed "Why" explanations.

3. Faculty Review & Audit

Faculty advisors review recommendations in real-time, approving or suggesting adjustments to build an empirical validation dashboard.
